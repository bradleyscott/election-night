How this is worked out

Marginleader minus second place, votes counted
Places outstandingtotal − counted, from the seat file
Vote still outballot papers issued at those places
Issued papers are an upper bound, not a forecast. They include papers that may be informal, and they count every voter issued paper at that place — including advance voters enrolled in other seats, whose votes count elsewhere. Special votes are excluded entirely. A seat shown as settled is arithmetic; a seat shown as cannot be called is not a prediction that it will change.
Updated every poll. Where a place's issued count is still zero, the bound widens rather than being guessed.
