Every seat below is the same 20,000 simulated finishes, read two ways: the chance each candidate wins, or where the final margin lands. Settled seats show the arithmetic that settles them; seats without enough count to model say so.

Both views come from one simulation per seat: 20,000 draws over the batches still to report, seeded from the scrape so every client sees identical numbers. The band is the 10th–90th percentile of the final margin; the tick is tonight's margin. Special votes are counted after the night and can still change a seat that is settled here.
