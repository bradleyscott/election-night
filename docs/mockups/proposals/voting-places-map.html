Where the count is

Four ways to show voting places and how much of them has been counted, on the map the electorates page already has. Status only: who is ahead is the seat fill's job, not the place mark's.

The map currently knows one thing about the count: how far the seat has got. The feed has recently published two more things — every voting place with a coordinate (votingplaces.xml, 2,686 places, 2,525 of them locatable) and, per place, when its result was received (e{NN}/votingplaces/*.xml, time_received, 9,363 place-results in total). This is the raw material for a map of the count itself.

One fact shapes every option below. A place reports its own seat's election-day votes, but the advance votes counted there go to the seats of the people who walked in. Auckland Central's count arrives from 69 places in 8 electorates — only 34 of them are inside its boundary. So a mark on the map is either national (all places, all seats) or scoped to a selected seat's contributing places, which reach outside the polygon.

1

Pips

One mark per place, filled when its result lands.

votingplaces.xml Needs a new roll-up
Counted Not yet counted Counted in the last 10 min
of voting places counted ·
Shows

The count as a wave: advance voting places all light at once just after seven, election-day places fill in seat by seat through the evening. Red marks are the last ten minutes, so you can see it moving rather than just how far it has got.

Needs

Coordinates are one request (617 KB). Status is not: receipt time lives in one small file per place-result, 9,363 of them. Polling all of those every two minutes is not realistic — a national version wants the Commission to publish a roll-up, or the collector to fetch places for the visible seats only.

Breaks when

2,525 marks at national zoom is texture, not data — Auckland and Wellington become a smudge at the two places people look hardest. The 161 unlocatable places never fill, so a seat that is 100% counted can still look unfinished.

2

Seat level

A bar at each seat's centre, filled by the share of its places counted.

Works today e{NN}.xml
All counted Past halfway Just started
label = voting places counted / voting places in the electorate
Shows

How far each seat has got, as a bar in its own party colour. The bar is the same height everywhere, so the levels compare across the map, and the seat's own name and 34 / 41 are set under it wherever the seat is wide enough to hold type.

Needs

Nothing new. total_voting_places_counted and percent_voting_places_counted are already in every electorate file the collector fetches; the counting fraction is one field on the payload.

Breaks when

The fill's opacity already means "size of the lead" in this map, so the mark has to carry the progress — and 64 marks over 2,525 places is a summary, not the count itself. A seat can also read ten of ten while advance votes from elsewhere are still arriving (see option 3).

Seat ledger

On selection: the places that carry this seat's count, inside the boundary and out.

Shows

Why a seat is not finished yet. Thirty-four places inside the boundary, thirty-five advance voting places in Epsom, Tāmaki and further afield that still count for this seat, and four mobile or care-home teams that have no coordinate at all — listed in the ledger, because they cannot be marked.

Needs

One directory listing and one file per place-result, fetched when the seat opens and refreshed only while it is short of places. Auckland Central is 69; the busiest seat is around 130. That is a few hundred KB, once, on demand — the same shape as the history request the detail panel already makes.

Breaks when

Marks outside the selected seat look like a bug unless the ledger says otherwise, and the map is zoomed to the seat, so the Epsom cluster is often off-canvas. It is also the only option that cannot answer "how is the country going" without a selection.

4

Night tape

The pace of the count against the Commission's own targets.

Works today Footer strip
Voting places counted · national / ·
Reported Still to come Commission target
50% by 10pm · 95% by 11:30pm
Shows

The half of election night a map is bad at: how fast it is going. One column per place receipt stacked as a running total, against the two targets the Commission publishes. The step at seven is the advance vote; the cliff after ten is the country finishing.

Needs

Nothing new: sum total_voting_places_counted across the electorates already polled and keep a few points of history, which the collector already writes to SQLite. Per-place receipt times would make the rug exact rather than sampled.

Breaks when

It says nothing about where, so it cannot stand alone — it is a footer under a map, or a strip on the front page. And a straight-line pace hides that the last 5% is special votes waiting weeks, not places still counting.

If it were my call

Gauge now, ledger on selection, tape in the footer.

The gauge is honest with today's feed and answers the question people actually ask — how much of the count is in. The ledger is where pips earn their keep: one seat, one fetch, and the explanation of why that seat is not done. The tape gives the night a pulse without asking anyone to read 2,525 marks.

The national pip map is the most beautiful of the four and the one I would not ship: it needs a feed roll-up that does not exist, and at the zoom people use it, it is weather rather than a count.

Ship first

Seat gauge — no new requests, one reducer field.

Then

Seat ledger — on-demand fetch, cached per selection.

Alongside

Night tape — footer strip on the map and the front page.

Deferred

National pips — blocked on a feed roll-up or a sampling plan.

Provenance. Boundary polygons are the shipped 2026 Stats NZ general electorates. Voting place coordinates and addresses are the real ones in the live 2026 feed (media.election.net.nz/xml/votingplaces.xml, read 2026-10-22); Auckland Central's contributing places are its real 69 place-results. Seat colours are the 2023 winners by nearest seat, and receipt times, issued-paper weights and counts are illustrative — the live feed is published all zeros until election night.

Receipt time. time_received is the field that makes any of this possible: it is stamped per place-result, so "counted" is not a guess. The 2023 archive carries the same schema, so the seat ledger and the tape can be built and tested against a finished cycle before the live one is published.
